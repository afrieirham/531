## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment — grounded surface, under-specified interaction.** The visual language could not be transplanted into an unrelated product unchanged: weights are the only loud elements, controls are instrument-like, one azure marks only active state, every numeric is tabular, and the composition literally renders the 5/3/1 artifact (three 40/50/60% warm-up chips, four week-rows 5s / 3s / 5/3/1 / deload, three set columns with the third carrying the AMRAP `+`). No athletic-brand tells. The gap is the IA of the glance: PRODUCT.md says success is "glance at the phone and know what to load next," but the app answers "what is the entire cycle for all four lifts." It has no representation of **now** — no current week, no current lift, no today-view. That temporal dimension is the one thing specific to the between-sets context, and it is the one thing the composition omits.

**Deterministic scan — 3 findings, 2 genuine.** CLI: `low-contrast` in dark mode (`#ffffff` on `#60a5fa`, 2.5:1) and an advisory `gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow` on the card. Browser overlay (light scheme): `low-contrast` on `.tagline` (`#64748b` on `#f1f5f9`, 4.3:1). The CLI and browser flagged **different** contrast pairs because the browser ran light-only. The detector caught the tagline failure the LLM review missed (the review measured `.tagline` at ~7.3:1 and was wrong; recomputed it is ~4.3:1, just under AA). The `gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow` advisory is the card's documented "Ambient Separator" — an intentional system choice, not a defect.

**Visual overlays:** the browser overlay ran successfully after a workaround (the preview browser is off the container loopback, so `localhost` failed and the page was reached via the tailnet IP, with the helper forwarded). It reported one light-scheme `.tagline` contrast finding. No reliable overlay persisted after the helper stopped.

## Overall Impression

This is a genuinely well-crafted single-purpose tool that has mistaken "show everything the program computes" for "answer what the lifter needs right now." The craft is real — tabular numbers, hairline discipline, one accent, offline, zero ceremony — but the central success metric (a glance between sets) is not met by the current IA, and two contrast defects plus a wrapped AMRAP marker undercut the numbers themselves. The single biggest opportunity: give the screen a sense of **now**.

## What's Working

1. **The North Star is realized, not just documented.** Live render confirms numbers are the only loud thing (weight text 14.07:1), chrome is hairline-quiet, and the single azure appears only on the selected pill and focus rings. It reads as an instrument panel, not a fitness app or a marketing dashboard.
2. **Exemplary numeric craft.** `tabular-nums` on the results grid means columns never jitter as 1RMs change — exactly right for a values reference.
3. **Zero-ceremony stateless loop true to the operating context.** Inputs and TM% persist in `localStorage`, the service worker registers, and the core "type a 1RM → correct 2.5 kg-snapped weights" path has no submit, login, or network dependency.
4. **Semantic baseline accessibility.** Native radios, wrapping `<label>`s, and a real `<table>` with `th scope="row"` are more than most calculators bother to do.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] No representation of "now": all four weeks shown flat, no current-lift focus.**
- *Why it matters:* The lifter's actual question is "what do I load for this set," but the app makes them hold "which week, which lift" in their head and scan ~76 numbers. It directly undermines the stated success metric.
- *Fix:* Add a current-week (default 1) 4-option selector and visually lead with that row (size/weight/azure tint); dim or collapse the other weeks behind an "all weeks" disclosure. At minimum add a sticky week indicator so the timed context is always on screen.
- *Suggested command:* `$impeccable shape`, then `$impeccable layout`

**[P1] Mobile breaks the documented single glance.**
- *What:* At 390px the page is ~2343px tall against an 844px viewport (2.8 screens); the Squat card starts at y≈1769. DESIGN.md claims no scrolling between cards.
- *Why it matters:* A one-handed user between sets cannot see her lift without scrolling — the exact behavior the design intends to prevent.
- *Fix:* Collapse warm-up and deload behind a per-card disclosure; show the current week by default and let "full cycle" expand; add a sticky exercise nav so the four lifts are one tap apart.
- *Suggested command:* `$impeccable layout` / `$impeccable adapt`

**[P1] Dark-mode selected TM pill fails WCAG AA (2.54:1) — detector-confirmed.**
- *What:* In dark mode `--accent` is `#60a5fa` but the selected segment label is hard-coded `#ffffff` (`src/style.css:100`). Measured white-on-`#60a5fa` = 2.54:1 (light mode passes at ~5.2:1).
- *Why it matters:* The one state a low-vision lifter must read — the active Training Max option — is the least legible text in the app in dark mode, likely the gym default.
- *Fix:* In dark mode set the selected label to `#0b1220` (dark text on bright azure), or keep the fill `#2563eb` with white text.
- *Suggested command:* `$impeccable audit` / `$impeccable polish`

**[P1] The AMRAP top set is the least legible element: the `+` wraps and is a bare muted glyph.**
- *What:* At 1280px in the 4-up grid the Set-3 cells are 67px wide and `.meta` ("85% × 5+") wraps, orphaning `+` onto line 2 on all four cards; `.weight`/`.meta` have `white-space: normal`.
- *Why it matters:* The AMRAP set is where the effort decision happens; breaking "5+" makes the most important prescription look broken.
- *Fix:* `white-space: nowrap` on `.weight`/`.meta`, a min column width, or drop to 2 columns below ~1360px; make AMRAP explicit with a small "AMRAP" badge or a bolder `+`.
- *Suggested command:* `$impeccable layout` / `$impeccable typeset`

**[P2] Silent blank state on empty/invalid 1RM, with layout shift.**
- *What:* `""`, `abc`, `0`, or a negative sets `results.hidden = true` and clears `innerHTML`; no message, and the card collapses to 0 height.
- *Why it matters:* The lifter cannot tell "no value" from "app broke," and the page reflows under their thumb.
- *Fix:* Keep the results region height stable and show an inline empty state ("Enter a 1RM to see your cycle"), plus a hint for non-positive values.
- *Suggested command:* `$impeccable harden` / `$impeccable clarify`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (impatient power user)**
- No keyboard path from entry to answer: no Enter-to-advance between the four 1RM fields, no shortcut to jump to a lift/week, no "show only current week." Alex must rescan the full matrix every set.
- No reset-to-defaults or bulk paste; changing the global TM% (y≈92) forces a scroll back to the top on mobile (content to y≈2295).
- `step="2.5"` arrow keys jump 2.5kg — fast, but with no styled stepper, undiscoverable.
- Positive: `localStorage` persistence means Alex never re-enters previous 1RMs.

**Sam (accessibility-dependent)**
- **Contrast:** dark selected pill 2.54:1 — the one active state fails AA. Other measured pairs pass (muted-on-card 6.76:1, weight 14.07:1). `.tagline` measures 4.3:1, also under AA.
- **Screen reader:** results are injected via `innerHTML` with no `aria-live`, so changing a 1RM announces nothing; the four `<section class="card">` elements have **no accessible name** because the exercise name lives in an `<input value>` rather than a heading — heading navigation cannot reach Bench/OHP/Squat.
- **Targets:** the Training Max segmented pills are 35px tall, below the 44px minimum; the visually-hidden radio is 13×13px. Inputs are a correct 44px.
- **Small type:** field labels and grid headers are 11.5px uppercase (contrast fine, size marginal for low vision).
- Positive: `:focus-visible` outlines on inputs and pills; inputs correctly label-associated; table uses `th scope`.

**Casey (distracted one-handed mobile)**
- **Thumb zone:** the TM toggle and all inputs sit at the top of the page/card; with the page 2.8 screens tall, reaching the Squat 1RM (y≈1808) one-handed is a stretch and its results sit below the fold.
- **State on interruption:** persistence restores entries, but there's no "you were here" cue — no current-week marker, no highlighted card — so Casey re-scans to relocate.
- **Keyboard interruption:** opening the mobile keyboard reflows the 2343px page and pushes live results further off-screen.
- **Visual glitch cue:** the wrapped AMRAP `+` and the 2+1 warm-up chips read as "unfinished," eroding trust mid-set.

## Minor Observations

- Documented-vs-implemented mismatch: DESIGN.md says the number spinner is suppressed, but there are no `appearance:textfield` / `::-webkit-inner-spin-button` rules (`appearance` computes to `auto`).
- "Every weight and percentage tabular" is only partially true: `tabular-nums` is on `.grid` only; `.chip b` and `.tm-line b` compute to `normal`.
- Redundant eyebrow: "TRAINING MAX" labels both the global toggle and each card's derived value.
- Card identity is weak: the exercise name is rendered inside a filled input styled identically to the 1RM field, so it reads as a form field, not the card title; at a glance all four cards look interchangeable.
- Warm-up chips wrap 2+1 at both widths, leaving an orphan chip.
- `--accent-soft` is defined but unused (documented as future capacity).
- Defaults (65/45/115/104) mean the app opens pre-populated; a first-run lifter must overwrite four values before the screen is theirs.

## Questions to Consider

1. If success is "know what to load next," why does the app answer "what is the whole cycle for everything"? Should the default state be one lift and the current week, with the full matrix one tap away?
2. Statelessness is a stated constraint — but is "which week am I in" *training state* (out of scope) or *session context the app must hold* to be usable at the rack? Where is that line, honestly?
3. The AMRAP top set is the decision-making set. Why is it the smallest, muted, and the only text that breaks across lines at 4-up?
4. If dark mode is the likely gym default and the selected TM pill is the one state you must read, why does the accent token flip to a lightness that fails contrast with its forced white label?
5. Does "single glance on a phone without scrolling" survive a 390px measurement (2.8 screens), or was that claim written from the desktop view?
